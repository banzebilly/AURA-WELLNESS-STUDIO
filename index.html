MEET YOUR THERAPIST

ITEC-Qualified Massage Therapist & Beauty Therapist

Welcome to AURA Wellness Studio. My name is Mulopwe Banze Billy, a qualified massage therapist and beauty therapist with seven years of hands-on experience in the beauty industry. I hold an ITEC qualification in massage therapy and a National Certificate in Beauty Therapy.

My training and experience cover massage, facials, nail care, waxing and exfoliation. I take pride in providing professional, hygienic treatments with care and attention to detail, making sure every client feels comfortable and well looked after.

At AURA, my goal is to create a welcoming space where you can relax, take a break from your busy day and enjoy treatments tailored to your needs. Whether you visit for a relaxing massage, a refreshing facial or your favourite beauty treatment, I want you to leave feeling refreshed, confident and cared for.
